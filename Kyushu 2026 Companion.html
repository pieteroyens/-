福岡

Fukuoka

30 Oct to 2 Nov · 17 Nov · 3 + 1 nights

The Basics Fukuoka

Two free days, Sat 31 Oct and Sun 1 Nov, plus the evening of Tue 17 Nov. The centre is Tenjin; Hakata station is about 2.5 km east, 5 minutes by subway, and the old temple town sits between them. Hours are for your dates.

Fixed points

  • Sat 31 Oct, 9:00 JST: book the Takachiho boat online.
  • Light-up walk, 31 Oct to 3 Nov, 17:30 to 21:00 (Gofukumachi-area temples until 21:30). Opening ceremony Sat 31 Oct, 17:00, Hakata Sennen-mon.
  • Express Pass exchange: on-site booth in front of Hakata station's central gate, 12:00 to 20:00, the only booth open before 17:00. Map

Kumamoto

2 to 5 Nov · 3 nights

OMO5 Kumamoto

Park the car once and leave it until 5 Nov. Several cafes close on Tuesday, so the holiday suits the castle, garden and malls, and Wednesday suits the cafes.

鹿児島

7 to 10 Nov · 3 nights

屋久島

Yakushima

10 to 13 Nov · 3 nights

Green Hotel, Anbo · then Miyanoura

Base in Anbo (south-east) on 10 and 11 Nov, then Miyanoura (north) on 12 Nov. Pick from the options below; only the ferries and the car return are fixed. Bring 800 yen each for the forest fees; sources differ between 500 and 800 yen.

Fixed points

  • Tue 10 Nov: ferry arrives Miyanoura 15:20; pick up the car and ask the office its closing time on 12 Nov. Anbo is a 30 min drive.
  • Wed 11 Nov is the best day for the long drives: the full island loop is about 100 km, roughly 3 h of driving without stops, and the last stretch is dark after 17:30.
  • Thu 12 Nov: check in near Miyanoura port, return the car at the harbour before the office closes, walk back.
  • Fri 13 Nov: walk to the port for the 10:00 ferry, arriving Kagoshima 12:45.

One full day, Sat 14 Nov. The boat and the kagura are fixed; everything else is a menu to pick from. The gorge, the station and the shrine are all within 10 min of each other by car.

Fixed points

  • Amaterasu Railway departures from Takachiho station: 9:40, 10:20, 11:00, 11:40, 12:20, 13:00, 13:40, 14:20, 15:00, 15:40; extra runs at 9:00 and 16:20 on busy days; no runs in rain; closed every 3rd Thursday. Tickets at the station, 2,300 yen plus 100 yen entry.

Current conditions

Everything in this plan was confirmed open, but three live situations can change things on the day.

  • July 2026 Kumamoto earthquake (shindo 7, 28 Jul): Kumamoto Castle reopened 13 Aug; most city museums reopened in August. Still closed: Kumamoto Museum and AEON Mall Kumamoto. The Kyushu Expressway reopened 14 Aug but has single-lane sections around Miyahara SA and Yatsushiro; add 20 to 30 min on 7 and 13 Nov. Takachiho boats may pause the day after an aftershock.
  • Aso: eruption alert level 2. The Nakadake crater rim is closed (about 1 km zone); gas levels were very high in October. Kusasenri, Daikanbo and Aso Shrine are open.
  • Sakurajima: alert level 3, frequent eruptions. Tourist areas near the ferry port are outside the 2 km zone; check ashfall and wind the day before.

Parking in cities

In Kumamoto, park once on arrival and leave the car until you drive to Aso on 5 Nov; every exit and re-entry restarts the fee. The steps below are general guidance for coin parking.

  1. Read the sign before entering: 空 means spaces free, 満 means full. Look for 最大料金 (daily cap). "24時間最大" counts 24 hours from entry; "当日最大" resets at midnight. For a multi-night stay you want a cap marked 繰り返し可 (repeats).
  2. Check the height limit (高さ制限) at multi-storey car parks, often about 2.1 m.
  3. Gate type: take the ticket at the barrier and keep it in your wallet, not in the car. Flap type: park in a numbered bay; a plate rises under the car after a few minutes. Remember the bay number.
  4. To leave, go to the pay machine (精算機): insert the ticket or enter the bay number, pay (cash always works; many take cards or IC cards), press 領収書 for a receipt.
  5. Drive out within a few minutes of paying (usually about 10) or the flap rises again.

Vegetarian note for restaurants

One traveller is vegetarian: no meat, ideally no fish or seafood as dishes; dashi, broths, eggs and dairy are fine.

お世話になっております。○月○日より○泊で予約しております○○と申します。 同行者1名がベジタリアンのため、お食事についてご相談させてください。 肉類（牛・豚・鶏、ハム・ベーコン等の加工品を含む）はいただけません。また、可能であれば魚介類も控えさせていただけますと幸いです。 なお、出汁やスープ類は問題ございません。卵・乳製品も問題ございません。 もう1名は制限ございません。 ご無理のない範囲で結構ですので、ご対応いただけますと大変助かります。どうぞよろしくお願いいたします。
  • Short phrase for restaurants: お肉とお魚なしで (without meat or fish). For ramen: チャーシューなしで (without chashu); pork broth is fine for you.
  • Safe lunches everywhere: soba, udon and tofu places, since dashi is fine.
  • Watch out in Kagoshima (black pork and fish dominate) and on Yufuin's Yunotsubo street (beef, chicken and eel hitsumabushi).
